Privacy policy

Your audio and app data stay on your device. SAMPLE-01 does not send audio, analytics, crash reports or other data off your device.

01 / SCOPE

About this policy

This policy explains how the SAMPLE-01 macOS app, developed by Zillatronics, handles your information. It applies to the app only, not to this website.

02 / PROCESSING

Your audio stays on your Mac

SAMPLE-01 works with the audio recordings you choose from your local collection. Library indexing, passage discovery, sample editing and WAV export take place on your device.

The app does not upload your recordings, samples, library information or working sessions to Zillatronics or any third party.

03 / DATA

No data collected through the app

SAMPLE-01 does not transmit personal information or other app data off your device. It does not send usage analytics, tracking information or automatic crash reports.

Zillatronics does not receive data from the app, so there is no app-collected data for us to sell, share or retain on our servers.

04 / STORAGE

You control your local files

Your audio library, locally saved app data and exported audio remain on your device. You choose which recordings to work with and can manage or delete your local files using macOS.

Because the app does not send us your data, we do not hold a remote copy of your library or working sessions.

05 / SCOPE

Services outside SAMPLE-01

Any data handling by macOS, an app store, or backup and sync services you use is governed by those services and your settings. This policy describes the SAMPLE-01 app itself.

06 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, we will update this policy to describe them. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
