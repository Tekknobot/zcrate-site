App privacy

How the SAMPLE-01 app handles your audio and project data.

This policy covers the app

This privacy policy applies to the SAMPLE-01 application. It describes the app’s handling of your audio and local project data. It does not describe the hosting or data practices of this documentation website.

SAMPLE-01 does not send your audio or other app data off your device. It does not send analytics or crash reports.

Audio and working data stay local

Audio indexing, analysis, auditioning and rendering take place on your Mac. The app reads the audio folders you choose and keeps its index, settings, project information and recovery data locally. Original recordings are not modified.

Saved projects can contain references to source files. Portable project exports include the audio needed to reopen the saved work. Those files are written to storage you choose; any later sharing or copying is your action.

Your files remain under your control

You choose the folders the app can read and where to save projects and exports. Use the app’s saved-work and recovery commands to locate local working files. Deleting a saved editor snapshot does not delete the original recordings.
