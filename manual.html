01 / LIBRARY

Getting started

SAMPLE-01 is designed for large sample collections, helping you find and audition passages without spending time loading each track into your DAW. You can use it with smaller collections, too.

Add a folder containing your existing audio recordings, then use the library indexing controls to scan a batch or your full collection. The left-hand Songs panel lets you search and choose source recordings.

Working locally. SAMPLE-01 is designed around recordings stored on your computer. Keep your original library backed up and test new releases on a small folder first.
02 / DISCOVER

Find passages

Use the Library panel to browse automatically proposed segments. Filter by passage length, tempo and musical key; choose the available key source when needed. Click a passage to audition it.

Passage timing, tempo and musical key are estimates. A candidate is not guaranteed to start at a perfect bar boundary or be musically suitable. Use your ears to decide.

03 / EDIT

Work in the sample editor

Drag a passage from the library into the sample editor. Change the editor length, audition the arrangement, loop playback and move or replace clips as needed. The editor also exposes pitch and tempo controls.

Editor boundary. In the current interface, clips may extend beyond the editor's chosen length. Playback and export follow the editor boundary, so increase the length to include those clips.
04 / PROGRESSION

Explore sample progressions

The Sample Progression area provides pattern choices and passage suggestions. You can audition a progression, then add it to the editor or replace the working sequence.

05 / OUTPUT

Export audio

Use Export editor WAV to render the current editor selection or Export WAV in the progression area to render that sequence. Check the chosen pitch, tempo and editor length before exporting.

06 / REFERENCE

Notes & limitations

Automatic slicing is heuristic. Musical-key and tempo estimates can be wrong, particularly with recordings that have shifting harmony or loose timing. SAMPLE-01 is a discovery and editing tool, not a replacement for critical listening or a full DAW.

When sampling commercially released recordings, you are responsible for obtaining any permissions required for the intended use of the resulting audio.
