# Screenshot capture notes

Captured 16 November 2026 from SAMPLE-01 1.1.0 (7), development source revision `a4dc2cd`. PNG dimensions: 1600 × 1000.

These are unaltered browser captures of the application's own local workspace. Native macOS File, Edit and Help menus sit outside this view. Captures used an isolated engine, index, recovery store and four generated demo recordings: Amber keys, Evening tones, Muted strings and Soft chord study. No personal recordings or projects were opened or changed.

- `01-editor.png`: an eight-bar sample idea and the passage library.
- `02-arrangement.png`: the demo workspace captured as a reusable pattern, with sample, synth and drum placements.
- `03-synths.png`: generated Poly notes and the voice controls.
- `04-drums.png`: an editable generated house pattern.
- `05-mixer.png`: arrangement playback meters; the drum group is collapsed to show the master alongside the synth channels.
- `06-library.png`: song search, a selected recording and the open indexing controls.

The capture environment and generated audio are temporary development tools, not part of the website or a downloadable app project.
